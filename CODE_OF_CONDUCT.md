# Boson Science Code of Conduct

Boson Science is committed to a constructive, respectful, and technically
rigorous community.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- treat other people with respect and assume good faith while evaluating claims
  on their evidence;
- welcome questions and differences in experience;
- give specific, actionable feedback about work rather than attacking people;
- acknowledge mistakes and correct them openly;
- respect privacy, confidentiality, and responsible disclosure boundaries; and
- represent research results, limitations, authorship, and provenance honestly.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, sustained disruption, plagiarism, fabricated evidence,
and knowingly misleading claims are not acceptable in Boson Science spaces.

## Scope

This code applies in organization repositories, issues, reviews, discussions,
events, and other spaces where someone represents Boson Science.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers or organization owners.
Do not use a public issue if doing so would expose a target, reporter, or private
information. Maintainers may edit or remove content, limit participation,
temporarily suspend access, or permanently remove a participant when necessary
to protect the community and its work.

Anyone handling a report must avoid conflicts of interest, share information
only with people needed to resolve it, and document the resulting decision.
Retaliation against a reporter or participant in a review is prohibited.
